KKK ka matlab kya hai?

KKK ka matlab hai Krishna, Kunal, Kalki — samay ke teen roop: bhoot (Krishna), vartaman (Kunal), aur bhavishya (Kalki). Yeh KKK Cinematic Universe (KCU) ka core concept hai.

KK full form kya hai?

Iss context mein KK, Kunal Kumar ka short form hai — jo KCU ke creator hain aur khud isi trilogy ke "vartaman" roop se jude hain.

KKK Cinematic Universe (KCU) kya hai?

KCU ek self-built Vedic mythology aur sci-fi universe hai, jisme paanch-part film arc, ek constructed language (ADIVANI), aur kai characters, novels aur design documents shaamil hain.
